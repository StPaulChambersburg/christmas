A Place Saved Just for You

Whoever shared this page with you wanted you to have a warm, welcoming place to celebrate this holiday season.

11:00 AM Traditional Service: A merry celebration of Christmas in the Sanctuary with traditional hymns, and the joyful sounds of our church choir.

7:00 PM Contemporary Service: Led by our Worship Team in the Family Life Center, we'll rejoice in the traditional story of the birth of Christ through a contemporary lens. With Christmas carols, old and new, and an activity center for young children and their families.

10:00 PM Traditional Service: A late-night service in the Sanctuary that encourages participants to reflect upon the good news that our Savior is born. We will sing Christmas carols, and enjoy a beautiful and serene performance from our Bell Choir, allowing for contemplation and introspection on the word of the Lord.

Come as you are. There is no dress code, and friendly volunteers will be at the door to help you find a seat.

FAQ

Where do I park?
There is ample parking located right in front of the Church.
What should I wear?
Whatever makes you feel comfortable! There is no dress code.
Is childcare available?
There is no childcare provided during Christmas Eve services, but children are invited to worship alongside their families in all services.
Where is the Sanctuary?
The Sanctuary is located through the big red doors. You'll see them right from the parking lot.
Where is the Family Life Center?
Looking at the building from the parking lot, the FLC is located to the right, through the glass doors under the portico.
