A Warm Welcome Awaits You

Whether you are a longtime neighbor or visiting family for the holidays, we would love to share this special evening with you. Our Christmas Eve candlelight service features traditional carols, an encouraging message, and the quiet beauty of a dimly lit sanctuary filled with candlelight.

Come as you are. There is no dress code, and we have dedicated friendly volunteers ready to help you find a comfortable seat.

What to Expect

What should I wear?
Anything from casual sweater and jeans to Sunday best—wear whatever makes you feel comfortable!
Is childcare available?
Yes! Nursery care (Ages 0–4) is available during our early service. Children are also warmly welcomed in the main worship sanctuary.
